I am a **third-year PhD student in biostatistics and applied mathematics** at the Institut Desbrest d'Épidémiologie et de Santé Publique (IDESP) in Montpellier, France. My thesis focuses on **modelling suicidal behaviour** using data from the French national health data system (**SNDS**), combining the analysis of health data and biomarkers, **time-series analysis**, random forests to identify important risk factors, and regression modelling.

Alongside my research, I teach **statistics tutorials** to physiotherapy students at the IFMK in Montpellier, covering descriptive statistics, statistical tests, linear and logistic regression and sample size calculation.

Before my PhD, I completed a master's degree in **computational biology and mathematics** at Aix-Marseille University as a CENTURI scholarship holder. During my master's internships, I studied the impact of **rainfall on the spread of typhoid fever in Mayotte** at the Institut Montpelliérain Alexander Grothendieck (IMAG), and the dynamics of **transposable elements** at the Laboratory of Genome and Development of Plants (LGDP) in Perpignan. I hold a bachelor's degree in mathematics from the University of Balamand, Lebanon.

My goal is to keep advancing research in **applied mathematics and statistics for public health**. More about my work can be found in the [publications]({{ base_path }}/publications/) and [talks]({{ base_path }}/talks/) sections, and in my [CV]({{ base_path }}/cv/).
